ABOUT OUR PAPER WEDDING VOW BOOKS
- The size of each book is 6 inches long and 4 inches wide or 7 inches long and 5 inches wide (your choice).
- Cover is made of premium heavyweight 140 pounds textured cardstock
- Inside the booklets are 6 sewn in pages (12 sides). Pages are 65 pounds and smooth, perfect for writing in.
- Each book will come with ribbon in the color of your choice
- Attention: These are printed with ink. It is not metallic. The color might appear slightly different due to different displays and different lighting. IF YOU FORGET TO LEAVE AN INK COLOR IN THE PERSONALIZATION BOX, WE WILL DEFAULT TO BLACK. The wording will appear exactly as shown in the photos (all lowercased and dated the same), unless otherwise specifically requested and noted in the personalization box by you.
