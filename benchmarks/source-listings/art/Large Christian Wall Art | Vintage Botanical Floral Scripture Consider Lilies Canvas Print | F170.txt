Vintage Oil Painting Printed on Cotton Canvas with Matthew 6:28 Added

-Description: Please view our video in the second photo to see close ups and further descriptions of this framed canvas art.

-Art is printed on a premium cotton canvas and permanently mounted to composite backer or birch (for the two larger sides), before being professionally framed. Art is lightweight and very easy to hang with the two sawtooth hangers that are mounted to the back.

-We have six frame options. Five of them are stained premium pine or painted gold metallic. We also have a hardwood oak option that is lightly oiled for a natural wood look. Please note for the oak frame option: As with all oak, the color can vary a little as it is oiled and not stained. The oil brings out the color of each individual piece of wood we use.
